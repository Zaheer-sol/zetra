# Go-to-market hypotheses

Zetra is for businesses that receive stablecoin payments and need those receipts matched to
invoices and recorded in their books. The first customer profile to test is a small service
business, agency, or online seller whose owner currently reconciles wallet activity manually.

These are hypotheses, not validated customer research:

- The recurring work is identifying the sender, finding the corresponding invoice, and recording
  the payment.
- Bookkeepers and accountants may be useful distribution partners if exports fit their existing
  workflow.
- A monthly software subscription may fit better than taking a percentage of receipts.
- A payment link may introduce the product to customers who also receive stablecoins.

## Initial validation

Interview business owners and bookkeepers about their current reconciliation process before
committing to a channel or pricing. Run a small product pilot using simulated payments first; do not
claim live-chain reliability until a funded transfer has been verified end to end against the
configured network, wallet flow, payment records and accounting reports.

Measure time to the first correctly matched payment, time spent reconciling, review rates, repeat
usage, and stated willingness to pay. Record observed results separately from assumptions.
